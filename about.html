WHAT WE'RE ALL ABOUT

Royal Harvest Junior School is a proudly Ugandan institution nestled in the heart of Mbarara, Kakyeka Road. We are a young and growing school built on the belief that every child deserves a quality education in a safe, nurturing, and inspiring environment.

Though we are new, our passion for excellence is not. From Baby Class all the way to Primary Seven, we are committed to giving every child the academic foundation, discipline, and confidence they need to thrive in school and in life.

OUR MISSION

Our mission at Royal Harvest Junior School is to provide a holistic and high quality education that develops the full potential of every learner. We are committed to nurturing academic excellence, strong moral values, and a love for learning in every child entrusted to our care.

We achieve this through dedicated teachers, an engaging curriculum, and a supportive school community that treats every child as royalty.

OUR VISION

Our vision is to become one of Mbarara's most trusted and outstanding junior schools — a place where children are not just taught but truly inspired. We aim to raise a generation of confident, creative, and responsible young Ugandans who are prepared for the challenges of tomorrow.

Within the next five to ten years, Royal Harvest Junior School aspires to be a centre of academic excellence and character development that parents in Mbarara and beyond are proud to call their children's school.
